# v0.6 — Sales Execution & Partner Workflow

## Goal
Turn the v0.5 Growth OS from a reporting/recording system into an execution system for sales and partner operations.

## Scope
- Explicit lead stage management with guarded transitions and stage history.
- Next-action workflow for leads: owner/action/due date visibility and overdue follow-up handling.
- Referral workflow clarity: create/referral link → click → referred lead → won deal → attribution.
- Commission workflow clarity: create commission → qualification → approval → payable → paid/void, with audit history.
- Operational dashboard views for pipeline movement, overdue work, partner conversions, and commission liabilities.
- Contact submission reliability: successful lead capture must produce a clear success response to the browser.
- Preserve MySQL/mysql2 production paths and Emerald-compatible deployment packaging.

## Acceptance
- No critical production errors.
- Contact form returns a success state when the lead is persisted.
- Lead stage changes are explicit, auditable, and reflected in dashboard/inbox.
- Referral and commission records expose a clear next action/status.
- CI passes lint, typecheck, production build, and Emerald standalone packaging.
- No Prisma CLI execution is required on Emerald.
