# P10.1 — Identity, Role & Workspace Authorization Hardening

## Objective

Make account identity, workspace membership, permissions and multi-business partner identity consistent across the entire authorization model before further roadmap expansion.

## Authorization model

### Account layer

`UserRole` defines the maximum capability of an account.

Account permission overrides may revoke capabilities but cannot elevate the account beyond its assigned role.

### Workspace layer

`WorkspaceUser.role` defines the user's capability inside one specific workspace.

The effective workspace permissions are:

`account-role permissions ∩ workspace-role permissions`

This means the same user can safely have different capabilities in different businesses.

Example:

- Business A: FINANCE
- Business B: AUDITOR

The user's account role does not override the Business B membership boundary.

### Platform administrators

SUPER_ADMIN and ADMIN may operate across active workspaces as platform administrators. Their workspace authorization context is treated as OWNER for workspace-scoped permission evaluation.

## Workspace role capability model

- OWNER: full workspace capability, capped by account role.
- ADMIN: full workspace capability, capped by account role.
- MANAGER: business operations, excluding account/workspace membership administration.
- SALES: sales execution.
- FINANCE: commission, payout and financial operations.
- AUDITOR: read-oriented oversight.
- CLIENT: limited client-facing visibility.
- PARTNER: partner portal capabilities.
- MEMBER: dashboard/basic membership.

## Partner identity boundary

Partner identity is network-level.

`Partner.workspaceId` may remain as home/registration metadata, but it is not a cross-business authorization boundary.

Commercial ownership remains:

- PartnerProgram.workspaceId
- CommissionContract.workspaceId
- ReferralLink.workspaceId
- LeadAttribution.workspaceId
- Order.workspaceId
- Revenue.workspaceId
- Commission.workspaceId
- Payout.workspaceId

A partner from Business A can legitimately participate in Business B when Business B's program and contract rules permit it.

## Implemented hardening

1. WorkspaceRole is now enforced by `authorizeWorkspacePermission()`.
2. Account-role permissions are the upper capability ceiling.
3. Permission overrides cannot elevate an account outside its UserRole.
4. Workspace membership role is returned as part of WorkspaceContext.
5. Platform administrators receive explicit OWNER workspace context.
6. Partner payment/order attribution no longer requires partner home workspace equality.
7. A dedicated partner attribution boundary helper documents and tests the rule.
8. Partner program discovery no longer assumes the partner's home workspace.
9. Account-role administration is restricted to platform administrators.
10. Workspace membership administration uses workspace-scoped authorization.
11. Existing-user account editing no longer accepts workspace membership fields.
12. User UI only exposes initial workspace/membership role during account creation.
13. Account permission controls cannot select permissions outside the account-role ceiling.

## Endpoint audit

Workspace-scoped administration must use `authorizeWorkspacePermission(permission, workspaceId)` or an equivalent authorization path that performs both account-role and workspace-role checks.

Audited/updated areas include:

- Products
- Product detail mutations
- Resources
- Partner administration
- Partner bulk provisioning
- Search/SEO profiles
- Workspace membership
- Orders
- Quotes
- Leads
- Customers
- Conversations
- Email operations
- Telegram operations
- Invoices
- Commission operations
- Partner risk/trust operations
- Catalog offers
- Partner programs
- Reseller/supplier administration
- Payout operations

Intentional exceptions:

- Partner portal endpoints use the partner's network identity and do not substitute the partner's home workspace for the target business.
- Public catalog/channel endpoints use their resolved business workspace and do not rely on internal user membership.
- Platform-level workspace creation is restricted to platform administrators.

## Isolation test coverage

Added tests verify:

- Finance account in Finance workspace can manage commission/payout operations.
- The same Finance account in an Auditor workspace cannot mutate finance.
- Sales accounts cannot elevate themselves through permission grants.
- Partner accounts cannot obtain internal partner-management or finance permissions.
- Client accounts remain read-limited.
- Auditor accounts cannot mutate commercial/financial data.
- One account can legitimately have different workspace capabilities.
- A partner whose home workspace differs from the commercial workspace can still attribute to the commercial workspace.
- A referral belonging to a different commercial workspace is rejected.
