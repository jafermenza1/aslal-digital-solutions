# P10.2 — Business Workflow UX, Role Workbench & Reporting

## Objective

Make the business workflow visible and usable through the UI while preserving the P10.1 authorization model.

Core rule: Account role defines the capability ceiling. Workspace role defines capability in the active business. The UI must use the effective intersection.

## Implemented

- Dashboard resolves active workspace and effective workspace permissions.
- Navigation consumes effective workspace permissions.
- Quotes and Orders use commerce.view.
- Active workspace selector is available in the dashboard shell.
- Task assignees are restricted to users belonging to the active workspace.
- Partner Portal no longer assumes partner home workspace equals commercial workspace.
- Workspace administrators can change workspace roles and remove memberships.
- A role-aware Reports Center is available.

## Partner boundary

Partner identity is network-level. Referral links, programs and commercial outcomes remain associated with their respective businesses. The partner portal presents approved business relationships and network-level attributable activity without exposing private customer information.

## Membership amendments

Workspace administrators can add, change workspace role, and remove membership. Account-level role editing remains separate. The schema does not contain a membership-level active flag, so membership status is not fabricated in the UI; user account activation remains account-level.

## Reports Center

The reporting entry layer exposes:
- Executive / Business
- Sales Pipeline
- Customers
- Commerce
- Marketing & Insights
- Partner Performance
- Finance
- Operations / Audit

Detailed record-level reports remain in the corresponding operational modules.

## Remaining refinement

- richer role-specific report generation/export formats;
- scheduled reports;
- comprehensive audit center;
- deeper partner performance drill-down;
- dedicated role workbench landing cards;
- automated two-business UI authorization tests.

## Runtime certification

Not executed by this change. Before merge run Prisma generation/migration deployment, typecheck, lint and tests, followed by controlled two-business authorization, attribution, task-assignment and report-isolation tests.
