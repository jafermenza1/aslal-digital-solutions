# Partner and Business Support Procedures

## Partner onboarding

Confirm identity, registration, partner type, business information, program admission, referral link, attribution policy, payout profile and terms acceptance.

## Attribution dispute

Collect order number, payment/provider reference, referral code, partner, campaign/program, timestamps and competing evidence. Do not change attribution without an auditable reason and actor.

## Commission question

Explain the attributed order, reconciled payment, program terms version, reward rule, refund hold, eligibility date and current commission state. Do not describe unreconciled commission as payable.

## Refund complaint

Verify provider refund reference and Aslal refund record. Distinguish partial and full refunds. Confirm resulting revenue and commission state.

## Payout support

Verify partner identity, payout profile, commission eligibility, amount/currency, payout status and provider reference. For failures, retain the original failure and process a controlled retry.

## Business/client support

Collect order number, payment reference, provider, amount, currency and timestamp. Never request passwords or payment credentials.

## Escalation

Financial duplication, foreign-workspace exposure, suspected account takeover and corrupted payout records are immediate Finance/Security escalation events.
