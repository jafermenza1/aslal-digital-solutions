# Omnichannel Lead Qualification Framework

Use this as a starting framework for WhatsApp, Telegram and web-form qualification.

## Core score

- +10: requested a general guide or educational resource
- +20: asked about implementation, pricing or timeline
- +30: used a calculator or diagnostic and requested a commercial next step
- +40: requested a quote, demo or payment integration discussion
- +50: supplied a concrete business problem, budget/timeline or buying authority

## Qualification questions

1. What are you trying to improve?
2. Which channel currently generates the demand?
3. What happens after someone enquires?
4. What system is currently used to record and follow up leads?
5. What would make the project successful?
6. Who needs to approve the project and when?

## Handoff rule

When a lead has a concrete problem plus a commercial signal, stop asking low-value questions and route the conversation to a salesperson with the conversation history, source and requested outcome.

## Suggested stages

NEW -> QUALIFIED -> FOLLOW_UP -> OPPORTUNITY -> QUOTE -> WON / LOST

Adapt fields and stage names to the CRM implementation.
