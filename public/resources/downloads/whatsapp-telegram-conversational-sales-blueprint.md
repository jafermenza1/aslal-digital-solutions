# WhatsApp & Telegram Conversational Sales Blueprint

## Conversation architecture
Traffic -> Conversation -> Qualification -> Human handoff -> Follow-up -> Opportunity.

## Qualification
Ask one useful question at a time:
1. What are you trying to improve?
2. What are you selling?
3. Where do enquiries currently come from?
4. What happens after someone enquires?
5. What timeline matters?

## Catalog bot
Each product/service response should include:
- clear outcome
- who it is for
- starting scope/price where appropriate
- one primary CTA
- human handoff option

## Human handoff rule
Handoff when the prospect has a concrete problem plus a commercial signal such as implementation timing, pricing request, quote request or buying authority.

## Automation safety
- Stop automated follow-up when the user replies.
- Record conversation context.
- Preserve owner and next action.
- Respect opt-out requests.
- Never fabricate product availability or payment confirmation.

## Suggested pipeline
NEW -> QUALIFIED -> FOLLOW_UP -> OPPORTUNITY -> QUOTE -> WON / LOST
